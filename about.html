About Us

Our goal was to transform culinary history into an interactive digital book experience, inviting users to discover, explore and connect with recipes from hundreds of years ago.

Ancient Recipes is a collaborative digital prototype developed by students from Billy Blue College of Design.

This prototype presents one complete recipe journey as a demonstration of the wider concept. In the future, the digital library could expand to include recipes from different cultures and historical periods, creating a growing archive where culinary traditions can be preserved, explored and shared.

The recipes preserve more than food, they carry the history, traditions and stories of the cultures they came from.

We recreated the familiar experience of exploring a library bookshelf, selecting a book, picking it up and reading through its pages. By combining this journey with animated pages, sound and storytelling, we maintained the historical feeling of a physical recipe book within an engaging digital experience.
